How CivicAI Works

From citizen reporting to transparent resolution.

1

Report

Citizens submit a civic issue with description, location and optional photo evidence.

2

AI Analysis

AI categorizes the issue and estimates its priority based on severity and community impact.

3

Community Vote

Other citizens can support the issue, helping identify problems affecting many people.

4

Resolution

The issue status can be tracked from reported to in-progress and finally resolved.

AI for a Smarter Nashik

The proposed system uses Artificial Intelligence and crowdsourced data to help prioritize civic problems and provide insights to administrators.

🧠 Automatic Classification
Identify whether an issue concerns roads, waste, water, drainage or other services.
🚨 Priority Prediction
Estimate urgency using severity, location, citizen votes and issue type.
📊 Civic Trend Analysis
Find areas with repeated complaints and identify recurring infrastructure problems.
🗺️ Hotspot Detection
Map clusters of civic complaints to support data-driven planning.
